SEO Slug Best Practices & Guidelines
  • Always use hyphens (-), never underscores (_): Search engines treat hyphens as natural word separators, but merge underscores into a single word string (e.g. red-shoes = 2 words, red_shoes = 1 word).
  • Target 3 to 6 descriptive words: Keep slugs between 12–55 characters. Avoid long sentences that dilute keyword density and risk URL truncation.
  • Strictly use lowercase letters: Linux/Unix servers (which power most CDNs and clouds) are case-sensitive. Lowercase filenames prevent 404 broken links and duplicate content indexing.
  • Strip stop words & filler words: Remove words like 'a', 'the', 'and', 'of', 'in' to concentrate search relevance on high-value topical keywords.
  • Avoid generic camera or screenshot names: Never publish raw names like IMG_2026.jpg or Screenshot-1.png. Always replace them with what the image actually depicts.
  • Prefer next-gen web formats (.webp, .svg, .avif): Search engines reward fast pages; WebP offers 25–35% smaller file sizes than traditional JPEG/PNG while maintaining crisp quality.
